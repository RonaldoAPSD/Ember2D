# Art credits

The RPG demo's tiles and characters come from two free packs by **Kenney**
(www.kenney.nl), released under **Creative Commons Zero (CC0)** — public
domain, free for any use, credit appreciated but not required:

- **Tiny Town** (1.1) — `assets/tilesets/town.png` (the pack's
  `Tilemap/tilemap_packed.png`, unchanged).
- **Tiny Dungeon** — `assets/tilesets/dungeon.png` (the pack's
  `Tilemap/tilemap_packed.png`, unchanged).

`assets/tilesets/extras.png` was made for this demo: one-pixel "walking
bob" frames of the Tiny Dungeon hero and wizard (plus plain copies, so an
animation clip can use both from one sheet), slash and sparkle effects, a
heart, a menu cursor, a shadow and the battle backdrop. Same terms: CC0.
